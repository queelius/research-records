# Two exact statistics of graph sandpile groups at nine vertices

Alexander Richard Towell's finite census gives **A395521(9)=76953** distinct
sandpile groups and **A401195(9)=177478** connected unlabeled simple graphs
whose sandpile group is cyclic, including the trivial group.

The [exact version 1.0.0 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23528939)
contains the source, complete group-frequency table, rejecting tests and
reproduction instructions with pinned inputs. A fresh replay checked all
261080 graphs using two Smith normal form algorithms at different roots and
exact determinants, after matching canonical inventory sets. All four public
file downloads were checked against local SHA-256 hashes.

Clancy, Leake and Payne already performed exhaustive graph-Jacobian
computations through nine vertices (arXiv:1309.3340v2, Remark 3.7). This is
not a first-census claim. McKay and Piperno and the dependency authors retain
their credits. Human mathematical review is pending; finite computation,
formal verification, novelty and OEIS acceptance are different claims.
Substantial OpenAI Codex assistance is disclosed.

The owning project is `oeis`; detailed research and OEIS editorial tracking
remain there. The public archive pins the exact source bytes, including
previously uncommitted files, without requiring the development repository.
The new terms were not submitted to OEIS at archival publication. Original
sequence disclosures through n=8 have separately recorded dates.
