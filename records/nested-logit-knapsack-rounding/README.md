# Uniform candidate families and knapsack rounding

Four complete accepted Lean proofs now establish a **single feasible family of
at most `(n+1)^2` assortments with a factor-two guarantee for every nonnegative
parameter**, together with actual fractional-knapsack optimizer construction
and both rounding inequalities. **Version 1.2.0 is archived at
[10.5281/zenodo.23529835](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23529835).**

Read [the technical note](NOTE.md), [exact scope and credit](record.json), and
[publication receipt](publication.json). The self-contained ZIP includes all
four accepted sources, three required platform definitions, pinned Lean/Mathlib,
the established verifier, independent full-target audits, logs and rejecting
controls. All four proofs reproduce from the final extracted ZIP. All three
public downloads match, and all 90 packet files are verified. No Prove2Me
account is needed to reproduce the archived sources.
