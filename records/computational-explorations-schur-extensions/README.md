## Contribution and evidence

The archived manuscript proves the finite-abelian-group doubling identity
`S(G,2) = 2 S(G,1)` exactly for odd-order groups and derives the two-color
split `32` versus `28` at order 49. Andrei Zabolotskii's observation and
Green–Ruzsa's one-color formula are explicitly credited.

It also gives `MS(3) = 16383`, with a written upper bound from the classical
Schur number `S(3)=13`. During October consolidation, the historical lower
coloring was restored to a public
[source path](https://github.com/queelius/computational-explorations/blob/100ebcd0b8745720ff3d4c79777a437f26b334f6/discoveries/schur-extensions/grid/ms3_witness_16383.txt).
Two enumeration orders checked all 64,449 unordered product triples; a
corrupted-color control was rejected. The
[verification receipt](https://github.com/queelius/computational-explorations/blob/100ebcd0b8745720ff3d4c79777a437f26b334f6/docs/audits/schur-archive-2026-10-20/verification.json)
contains the command, output, and hash.

All three public manuscript files were downloaded and matched against the
preserved author files; see the [archive receipt](publication.json).
The [owning-project handoff](https://github.com/queelius/computational-explorations/blob/100ebcd0b8745720ff3d4c79777a437f26b334f6/docs/schur-archive-handoff-2026-10-20.md)
tracks remaining source integration and review.
