# Small-order C6 saturation

Alexander Richard Towell, with substantial Claude and OpenAI Codex assistance.

The exact saturation values at orders 11--15 are 14, 16, 17, 19, 20.
Their complete minimum families contain 1, 35, 4, 205, and **24**
isomorphism classes. The order-15 classification is the v6 addition:
twelve classes contain leaves and twelve are leaf-free.

The [v6 archival record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23527267) was published
on October 21, 2026. All three public files match the verified local bytes;
the receipt is in `publication.json`. This is the public disclosure date
for the newly archived classification; computation dates remain separate.

The archive supplies the paper, exact graph6 families, historical lower-bound
logs, leaf attachments, independent weighted-kernel recovery, complete
rejection transcripts, source, controls, and reproduction instructions.
Development remains in the private `open-problems` repository; its visibility
is unchanged. The scoped public source is in the archive. [HoG 58092](https://houseofgraphs.org/graphs/58092)
is the existing order-15 witness; the complete family is archived together.

The order-9/10 values and families, and the order-12 value, are prior work by
Zhang, Luo and Shigeno. The checked order-11 witness corrects their reported
small-case value 15. Lan, Shi, Wang and Zhang already supplied matching upper
constructions at orders 12--15. The evidence distinguishes these precedents
from the exhaustive lower bounds and classifications.

Completeness relies on the stated generation and partition contracts and
written kernel-coverage proof. Matching counts do not alone prove it.
Independent checking refers to separate algorithms and implementations;
human peer review and proof-assistant verification are not claimed. The
prefix pilot records a limited negative compression result.
