# House of Graphs witness ledger

Alexander Richard Towell's ledger of **26 House of Graphs witness graphs**:
C6, C5, K4, P4 and P5 saturation and three Zarankiewicz z(m,n;3,4) graphs.
Each byte-exact graph6 witness is accepted only when two code-independent
implementations, pure Python and NetworkX, verify its defining property.

The [exact version 1.0.1 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23633619)
is pinned to [tag v1.0.1](https://github.com/queelius/hog-contrib/tree/v1.0.1)
and source commit `3ce5212f7e9f0a0e3bdda187e60d0edb37348b48`. It archives the completed prior-art
assessment and corrections to eight HoG annotations. No graph, HoG ID,
verification parameter or backing evidence DOI changed from version 1.0.0.
This is a ledger correction, not a new mathematical contribution.

All records are now assessed: 17 known values, 6 new values,
2 classifications and 1 correction; none remain unassessed.
Those labels have dated, bounded prior-art support, not a global novelty
certificate. A witness establishes attainment; exactness additionally needs
the lower-bound evidence cited by its record. See also the
[C6 saturation record](../small-order-c6-saturation/README.md).

A fresh archive installation passed all 65 tests and verified all 26
witnesses with both checkers. Generated files are current; a deterministic
rebuild and the public download match the recorded SHA-256. Source statement
coverage is 631/662 (95.32%); branch coverage was not measured.
The [1.0.0 record snapshot](versions/1.0.0/record.json) preserves the earlier
index bytes and claim counts; its public archive remains unchanged.

House of Graphs, prior authors and dependency authors retain their credit.
Substantial Claude Code and OpenAI Codex assistance is disclosed. There is
no human peer-review or proof-assistant verification claim. Detailed
development remains in the owning `hog-contrib` repository.
